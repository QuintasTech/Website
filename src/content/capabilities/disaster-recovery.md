    text: Regulators, insurers and boards asking for evidence of recovery objectives being met.

  - title: Business impact and DR assessment
    text: Map critical applications, dependencies and recovery objectives (RPO and RTO) to what the business can tolerate.

  - title: Data center and DR room relocation
    text: Full discovery and inventory, move planning, de-install, secure transport, rack and stack, cabling and cutover.
  - title: Replication and backup
    text: Continuous replication and immutable backup so data is protected and recoverable.
  - title: Failover testing and runbooks
    text: Scheduled recovery tests, documented runbooks and reports you can hand to auditors.
