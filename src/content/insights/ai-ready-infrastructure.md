Many AI pilots succeed in a sandbox and stall on the way to production. The cause is often not the model but the foundations underneath it.

## Compute

Training and inference have very different needs. Decide which you will run, where, and whether GPU capacity belongs on-premises, in the cloud, or both.

## Storage and data

AI is only as good as the data it can reach. Fast, well-governed storage and reliable pipelines matter more than the choice of model.

## Network

Moving large datasets between plants, data centers and the cloud needs bandwidth and low latency planned in advance.

## Governance

Know where sensitive data lives and who can use it before it is fed to a model.

A short readiness assessment answers these questions and turns a promising pilot into a production roadmap.
