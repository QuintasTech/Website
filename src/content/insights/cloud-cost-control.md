Cloud bills rarely grow because of one bad decision. They grow through hundreds of small ones. These five steps tackle the most common sources of waste.

## 1. Tag everything

You cannot manage what you cannot attribute. Enforce tags for owner, environment and cost centre at deployment time.

## 2. Rightsize regularly

Most workloads are provisioned for a peak that never arrives. Review utilisation monthly and resize.

## 3. Schedule non-production

Development and test environments rarely need to run nights and weekends.

## 4. Commit where it is predictable

Reserved instances and savings plans cut costs on steady workloads. Keep variable workloads on demand.

## 5. Make cost visible

Share dashboards with the teams that create the spend. Visibility changes behaviour faster than policy.
